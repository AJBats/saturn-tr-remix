All variants

Flicker: mean change of a frame from the last, less the reference's change, in luminance (0 to 255): what moves on screen that the scene doesn't. Error: mean difference from the reference, still or not. Corner error: how far the room corners on screen land from where an exact projection puts them, the 99th percentile (whole pixels alone allow up to 1.41). Vertex wobble: how much a room corner's offset from its exact position changes from one frame to the next, in pixels, the 95th percentile of the corners on screen (whole-pixel corners alone reach about 1 px). VDP1 ms: the rooms' drawing time with the calibrated Saturn costs (a 30 fps frame is 33.4 ms; the rooms alone, without Lara or objects). Click a row to show it in panel B.
