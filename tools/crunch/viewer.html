Per frame

Flicker: mean change of a frame from the last, less the reference's change, in luminance (0 to 255): what moves on screen that the scene doesn't. Variants that change the art are held to the reference drawn with their own art. Lower-contrast pictures flicker less for that reason alone, so read the shading variants' flicker with their detail. Fine: the frame's finest detail (mean Laplacian, pixel to pixel) over the reference's: above 1 crunchier than the ideal, below 1 softer. Form: its middle-sized detail (difference of 1 and 3 pixel blurs: cracks, edges, lumps) over the reference's: below 1 blobby or washed out. Error: mean difference from the reference (additive light, as the Saturn); error × from the reference lit as the PS1 multiplies. Corner: how far room corners on screen land from an exact projection, 99th percentile (whole pixels alone allow 1.41). Wobble: how much that distance changes frame to frame, 95th percentile. VDP1 ms: the rooms' drawing time at the calibrated Saturn costs (a 30 fps frame is 33.4 ms; rooms alone, no Lara or objects). Cmds: VDP1 commands (each a face, so CPU work too). Tex KB: the frame's textures, as drawn (VRAM). Grain, lonely, run: the room textures themselves, weighted by the screen they cover here: grain is the texel-to-texel luminance step over the texture's contrast (how much of its contrast is noise rather than shapes), lonely the share of texels unlike all four neighbours, run the mean length of same-colour runs. Sonic R's row gives its values.
