# Draw order hints, made into build/gen/depth_hints.h by tools/depth_hints.py.
#
# For the few places where the depth sort shows something through what hides
# it. Each applies while Lara stands in a block of a room ("room bx bz"; a
# block is a square of the world's 1024-unit grid, x >> 10 and z >> 10), or
# anywhere ("*"). A thing is an item (its index in the level) or a room's
# static mesh (room:index). Then one of
#
#   buckets                     its faces sort that many ordering table buckets
#                               farther (positive) or nearer (negative)
#   behind room R qN...         all its faces are drawn before those faces of
#                               room R, a quad (q) or triangle (t) each, that
#                               face the camera: for a thing wholly behind
#                               their plane, which they always hide
#   mN qN behind room R qN...   the same for one face of it, of its mesh N
#                               (from its first)
#
# The polygon hunt (debug menu) reads off all of these: Lara's room and block,
# the face drawn "over" and the one "under" it.
#
# level       where          thing          hint

# the tilted plank (BRIDGE_TILT) at the far edge of the top of the raised
# ledge beside the bridge (hunt: its q3 and q2 from blocks 24,55 and 24,56).
# It slopes through the ledge top's plane, so only the two faces seen through
# the ledge, not the whole plank.
LVL_TR1_1     *              item 14        m0 q3  behind room 13 q101
LVL_TR1_1     *              item 14        m0 q2  behind room 13 q101

# ... and the same at the second bridge (hunt: from room 15 block 18,45)
LVL_TR1_1     *              item 20        m0 q3  behind room 13 q92
LVL_TR1_1     *              item 20        m0 q2  behind room 13 q92

# the icicle hanging in room 14 under room 13's floor blocks 30,48 and 29,48
# (hunt: from blocks 29,48 29,47 28,47)
